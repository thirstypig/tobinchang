Ideas for this page

No wrong answers here. Pick one that sounds fun and start there — you can always change it later, and probably will.

  • What you're into right now, and why
  • Photos you've taken
  • Reviews — games, films, albums, places you've eaten
  • A running list of things you're learning
  • Stats, highlights, or write-ups from your season
  • Short writing, or a proper blog if you've got things to say
  • Projects you've built, with pictures
  • Books you've read this year
  • Your current playlist, swapped out every month
  • A "now" page — literally just what you're up to lately

A personal site doesn't have to be impressive. It has to be yours. The strange, specific ones are always better than the polished ones.
